Nepal is a beautiful landlocked country in South Asia, rich in natural beauty and culture.

It is home to the world’s highest peak, Mount Everest, attracting travelers and climbers globally.

Kathmandu, the capital city, is filled with ancient temples, stupas, and vibrant festivals.

The people of Nepal are known for their hospitality and the saying "Atithi Devo Bhava" — guests are like gods.

From the Terai plains to the Himalayas, Nepal’s geography is incredibly diverse and breathtaking.
