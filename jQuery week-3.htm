Nepal is a beautiful landlocked country in South Asia, rich in nature and tradition.

It is home to Mount Everest, the world's highest peak, and a paradise for adventure seekers.

Kathmandu Valley showcases centuries-old temples, art, and vibrant festivals.

The Nepalese people are known for their kindness and the motto "Atithi Devo Bhava" — guests are gods.

From lush Terai plains to towering Himalayas, Nepal’s landscapes are breathtakingly diverse.
